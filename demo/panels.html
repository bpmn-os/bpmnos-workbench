      Both tabs over stores fed with a hardcoded report rather than with a run, side by side because they
      are built from the same classes and must look it. The first performer is an ad hoc sub-process, idle,
      with three tokens waiting; the second is a sub-process conducting one, which stands at the head of its
      list as a fixed entry and carries no arrows; the third is a process standing idle with nothing under
      it. Move a row with ▲ and ▼ and watch the order above: that is what a later report is read against.
      Nothing passes the fixed entry, and the divider is a row of the list like any other, so moving it is
      the same act as moving a token past it. Tick a token to select it and the filter of either heading
      narrows to what that selection concerns.
