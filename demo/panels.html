      Both tabs over stores fed with a hardcoded report rather than with a run, side by side because they
      are built from the same classes and must look it. The first performer is an ad hoc sub-process, idle,
      with three tokens, the first of them performed and kept as a greyed record carrying the offer to forget
      it; the second is a sub-process conducting one, greyed where it stands and carrying no arrows; the
      third is a process standing idle with nothing under it. Move a row with ▲ and ▼ and watch the order above: that is what a later report is read against.
      Nothing passes the fixed entry, and the divider is a row of the list like any other, so moving it is
      the same act as moving a token past it. Tick a token to select it and the filter of any heading
      narrows to what that selection concerns.
    

    

      The Decisions tab holds a decision task per row, drawn with the symbol `bpmnos-js` draws it with on
      the canvas and marked with the token standing at it. Expanding one shows what that token holds and
      then its choices, each as a name above the control that takes it. Choose a mode on the first and the
      duration beneath it opens with the bounds and the step that mode allows; choose a duration and the
      paper plane is offered; then change the mode to one whose bounds no longer admit that duration and
      watch the duration clear and the offer withdraw. A choice that cannot yet be made is shown all the
      same, so the reader sees how many the task requires.
